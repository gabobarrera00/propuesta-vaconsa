Site development system

Everything needed to bring water and drainage infrastructure to a development, from the main network to each lot: sanitary sewer (house connections, manholes, manhole frames), potable water (Imperial, Metric and C900 systems) and storm drainage (corrugated polyethylene pipe, catch basin grates).

Sewer pipe

Compliant with NMX-E-215 and the NOM-001-CONAGUA-2011 watertightness standard. Three stiffness series depending on the dead load of the installation.

Sewer fittings

Injection-molded in a single piece, brown, to NMX-E-215/2, with an integrated elastomeric ring and spigot-bell or bell-bell design for installation and line continuity. Unaffected by sanitary sewer gases, including sulfuric acid, and by aggressive acidic or alkaline soils — excellent corrosion resistance.

Storm drain system — corrugated polyethylene pipe

High-density polyethylene with a corrugated exterior and smooth interior wall, for collecting and conveying stormwater in urban developments, with high structural stiffness and easy installation.

Potable water system

From the water treatment plant to each home: primary network, booster station and secondary network. PVC families available in the Imperial system (RD26 · RD32.5) and the Metric system (Class 7 · Class 10).

Fittings and valves

Ductile iron and cast iron (FoFo) flanged fittings, air release valves, flow control gate valves, check valves and flow regulating valves.

Imperial-series pressure pipe

C40, DWV C40 and standard sanitary pipe, to NMX-E-145 (unplasticized PVC for pressurized water supply, Imperial series) and the NOM-001-CONAGUA-2011 watertightness standard. For potable water networks — main and secondary lines — irrigation systems and treatment plants.

Building

PEX / CPVC pipe — indoor

Hot and cold water inside the building. PEX is zoned by area with manifolds; CPVC CTS resists chlorine corrosion and is easy to install.

Fire protection system

UL/FM grooved-end steel piping and grooved couplings, instrumentation and monitoring (pressure gauges), control and sectional valves, supply and pumping, and discharge points: hose cabinets, automatic sprinklers and fire department connections.

Structural steel

G-56 and G-42 rebar, HSS tubing, structural and mechanical tubing, ASTM A36 and A572 angle, C/Z purlins (Montén) Gr33 and Gr50, PTR rectangular tubing.
