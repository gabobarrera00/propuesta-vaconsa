About us

Four decades supplying the energy, steel, chemical, food, pharmaceutical, manufacturing and OEM industries.

Vaconsa Productos Industriales began operations on February 17, 1978 under the company name Válvulas y Conexiones del Norte, S.A. de C.V., distributing carbon steel, stainless steel and specialty valves, pipe and fittings.

Over the years we have built solid relationships with customers and suppliers, which allows us to hold a broad inventory and meet the real demands of the industrial market in northeastern Mexico.

We sell high-quality products with direct factory support, and every shipment is delivered with its corresponding certificate.
